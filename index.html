How I approach automation

A simple loop behind every machine.

Whether it is a box-forming robot or a filling line, the control loop is the same. Keeping to it makes machines easier to test, hand over and maintain.

01 · Input

Sense

Sensors, switches and operator inputs tell the controller the state of the machine.

02 · Logic

Decide

PLC logic runs the sequence and manages faults and safety interlocks.

03 · Output

Act

Robot arms, VFD-driven motors and actuators carry out the task.

04 · Feedback

Report

Indicators and the operator panel show line conditions at a glance.
